# Attackers — 1v1 Pricing

Rating is for **1v1 custom vs one human defender**. Format: op — 1v1 tier —
why — how it dies. Ops not listed = fine but no 1v1 edge.

## S — find him, then it's over
- **Jackal** — S — footstep scan on a SOLO defender is brutal: every ping
  is him, no decoy teammates. Counters roamers (D2/D3 families) almost
  unfairly. Expect this to become his permanent ban once used; have the
  fallback ready. Dies to: pure anchors who never move.
- **Lion** — S — EE-ONE-D freezes a lone defender: move = wallhack ping,
  freeze = you walk a known-still target. Use to cover YOUR entry moment or
  a late-round objective touch. Dies to: patient anchors (drone them
  instead).
- **Iana** — S — the clone is a free dry-peek every fight and a third
  "drone". Solo defender must respect every peek. The safest gunfighter's
  intel op.
- **Zero** — S- — persistent cams from unexpected angles + good gun; your
  "teammate eyes" while you push. Claymore covers the flank a teammate
  would.
- **Dokkaebi** — S- post-rework — vs one defender, phone/loud-ping
  pressure localizes him and denies his cam time. VERIFY current Jegeo
  Payload mechanics (Y11S2 rework) before first use.

## A — strong solo value
- **Nomad** — A — airjabs are your flank watch: seal the path behind you
  and clear the rest of the map 1-directional. A downed-by-airjab defender
  in 1v1 is usually a lost fight for him.
- **Twitch** — A — shock drone kills his cams/gadgets AND scouts; strips
  the defender intel-net (counters Valk/Melusi/Maestro nets).
- **Grim** — A- — bee swarms give persistent position pings on a roamer
  without exposing you; good clock-squeeze tool.
- **Buck / Sledge** — A- — solo vertical: open soft floors/ceilings over
  his hold and shoot the ping, no teammate needed. Core of punishing
  confirmed anchor habits.
- **Fuze** — A- — into a CONFIRMED objective-anchor read only: cluster the
  room he sits in, walk in after. Ugly, effective, tilts brothers. Don't
  overuse (conditioning burns it).
- **Zofia / Ash** — A- — concussion/breach round starts the fight on your
  terms; pick when the plan is "win the 50/50 with utility first". Ash =
  pure gun pick.
- **Ying** — B+ — candela entry vs a known position; loud, telegraphs —
  save for big rounds after conditioning slow play.
- **Deimos** — B+ — DeathMARK finds him BUT reveals you too; in 1v1 the
  mutual-intel trade favors the better gunfight — pick only when you're
  confident winning the duel and just need to force it. Revolver spike.
- **Ram** — B+ — BU-GI opens his floor/cover without exposure; slower solo
  but a real anchor-punisher alternative to Buck.
- **Gridlock** — B+ — trax = one-way doors behind you; the lazy-safe flank
  watch for slow defaults (A1/A3).

## B — playable with a plan
- **Thatcher / Thermite / Ace / Hibana** — B — hard breach matters only
  for a specific angle you pre-planned (e.g. open garage wall to stretch
  his anchor). Ace's ranked 80% ban rate is 5v5 logic — don't copy it.
- **Montagne / Blitz** — B — a solo defender with no teammate crossfire
  struggles vs shields… until he picks nitro/Noor (post-Sept 1). Use
  sparingly, expect the counter-adapt within 2 rounds; log which counter he
  reaches for.
- **Osa / Sens** — B — solo plant-cover tools; value depends on house rules
  including defuser. Ask rules first.
- **Brava** — B- — converting his gadgets is great but drone-piloting time
  is exposure time with no cover; only vs heavy-gadget setups (Kapkan
  spam read).
- **Glaz** — C+ — only on maps/rules with long outside sightlines; a
  window-watching brother read makes it a cheese round.
- **Striker** — B — flexible generalist, gun + utility freedom; the "no
  read yet" default when you just want a clean gunfight with flashes.
