# Defenders — 1v1 Pricing

Rating is for **1v1 custom vs one human attacker**, not ranked. Format:
op — 1v1 tier — why — how it dies. Ops not listed = fine but no 1v1 edge.

## S — round-winning alone
- **Pulse** — S — wallhack vs exactly one heartbeat; nitro + soft floor =
  duel over without a gunfight. Below-obj hold is the signature line. Dies
  to: being pre-banned, attacker holding range/verticality.
- **Vigil** — S — invisible to the attacker's ONLY intel source; drones die
  or learn nothing. Roam/ambush king. Dies to: sound discipline (he still
  makes noise), Jackal/Lion-style scans.
- **Mozzie** — S — steals the attacker's drones: you gain intel AND blind
  him in one gadget. Pest a prep-phase drone every round. Dies to: attacker
  droning cautiously/leaving drones outside.
- **Kapkan** — S — 5 lethal-ish traps nobody will spot for him; solo
  attackers under time pressure run doorways. Dies to: slow methodical
  clearing, drone-first habits (track brother's trap-spotting rate).
- **Frost** — S — a mat downed attacker in 1v1 is a WON ROUND (no teammate
  revive-deny needed — walk over and finish). Windows + behind-shield mats.
  Dies to: pre-aimed known spots; vault-bait awareness.

## A — strong solo value
- **Mute** — A — jams drones at chokes → blind entries; also denies
  Dokkaebi-style pings. Pairs with any ambush family (D2/D3).
- **Valkyrie** — A — three extra eyes replace teammates; hold off-angle and
  peek only on cam confirmation. Dies to: him finding cams (log which cams
  he's learned).
- **Lesion** — A — free intel ping + slow on every step he takes; scales
  with round length. Great with clock-weaponizing (D1/D4).
- **Ela** — A — concussion mines cover your back while you hold one angle;
  wins the re-peek war.
- **Melusi** — A — banshees are sound alarms + slows he must SHOOT (noise =
  free info). Anchor-net core.
- **Caveira** — A- — pure 1v1 predator on silent step; high risk high
  reward, ONLY into an aggressive/impatient read (tilt rounds). Interrogate
  value is nil (no team) — she's here for Silent Step + Luison ambush.
- **Fenrir** — A — solo attacker has no teammate to eat/clear F-NATTs;
  forces slow, blind pushes into your crosshair. Expect him to ban it once
  it beats him.
- **Solis** — A- — sees his drones/gadgets through walls → clean drone
  kills and safe repositioning. (Verify current Solis nerf state before
  leaning on prep-phase usage.)
- **Alibi** — A- — prismas punish exactly the twitchy pre-fire a brother
  duel produces; each shot at a decoy = position ping + free swing.

## B — playable with a plan
- **Smoke** — B+ — plant-denial value shrinks if house rules skip defuser;
  still the best "hold one door forever" gas in clock-weaponized D1. Ask
  the rules first.
- **Jäger** — B+ — ADS value low solo (fewer nades thrown at you), but the
  carbine + P-10C make him a pure-gunfight pick when you just want to
  out-shoot him.
- **Bandit** — B — batteries irrelevant vs no hard breach; pick = 3-speed +
  gun + Keratos rotation holes for D3 roams.
- **Maestro** — B — evil eyes annoy solo attackers (no Thatcher partner),
  but big and loud; anchor-only.
- **Echo** — B — yokai stall + intel is decent, drone piloting eats your
  own attention with no teammate covering you.
- **Azami** — B — ranked S-tier, 1v1 B: kibas reshape angles you fight
  from, but her value is mostly team site-holds. Fine, not a crutch.
- **Warden** — C+ — only into a flash-heavy brother read (Ying/Blitz
  habits).
- **Mira** — C+ in 1v1 — one-way intel is nice but static; a solo attacker
  just goes elsewhere. Don't waste bans on her; don't crutch her.
- **Noor** (Y11S3, Sept 1) — unpriced. If brother plays shields, she likely
  becomes the auto-answer. Refresh meta file at season start.
