route confidence), current YouTube guides — Cheezit "Setup and Defend Every

## The one-line doctrine
**Route-first, gadget-second.** Gio's body goes to the same places; his
operator changes. Predict the route, pick the operator that punishes it, and
NEVER repeat the exact punishment after it works (vary ≥2 of: operator,
spawn, entry side, tempo, position, gadget placement, plant side, fallback).

## Fight permissions (both sides, every round)
- GREEN: pre-aim advantage, distraction active, plant/defuse action, side or
  back angle.
- YELLOW: both aware — fight only with cover/strong geometry.
- RED: fresh breach hole, revenge chase, trap-sound swing, known held
  doorway, aim-duel with no objective value. Do not volunteer RED fights —
  vs Gio's aim they are coin flips you lose.

# A. DEFENSE: Basement (Laundry/Supply) — vs his Laundry attack

**Prediction:** he comes down Laundry Stairs into Laundry. Do NOT defend as
if Freezer/Blue are equally likely. Do not be a static Supply anchor.

### A1 — Primary: MOZZIE (new to him here; poisons his drone-heavy attack)
- Setup (prep): pests placed to catch drones at the Laundry approach — one
  covering the Laundry Stairs doorway area, one inside Laundry near the
  Supply connector. Reinforce per standard: Laundry↔Freezer wall LEFT half
  reinforced, RIGHT half head-holes (your swing line); Supply Closet
  connector = jumpable head-hole vault; rotate between Laundry and Supply.
  Keep Electric/Freezer hatches shut.
- Position: OFFSET, Supply/Freezer-side, holding the post-stair second
  crossing through your head holes — never the raw stair duel.
- Trigger discipline: a drone alone is NOT the execute. React when his BODY
  commits down Laundry Stairs. If you capture a drone, take a 3-second look,
  then get off the cam and reset — never sit on it.
- C4 in hand for the moment he commits into the Laundry choke.
- Win state: he pushes his favorite route blind, eats the crossing angle or
  the nitro; or he abandons Laundry for a lane he hasn't practiced.
- IF he switches to Fuze/Grim (unbanned): the one-position trap is dead —
  move laterally IMMEDIATELY on the first drill/swarm sound, rotate through
  Supply toward Blue/Bunker side, make the fight happen one room later.

### Changeups after A1 works (in order)
- A2 **Smoke**: let him KNOW the route — deny the timing. Gas the Laundry
  choke only when his body commits, fight the second crossing. (Same
  position family, different denial — he preps for pests, meets gas.)
- A3 **Kapkan or Frost** on the Laundry lane + off-site hold deeper in
  Supply: punishes the fast, tilted version of his push that follows two
  failed slow ones.

# F. In-round decision rules (recite before every round)
1. Droned → move one cover / change the expected angle before his execute.
2. Fresh breach → never volunteer the peek.
3. Displacement utility (Fuze drill sound, Grim swarm) → lateral/deeper
   IMMEDIATELY, never re-peek the same line.
4. Last 30s (defense) → collapse to objective. Last 15s → gun only.
5. Passive utility fired = information, not permission to swing.
6. Attack: chase nothing. A displaced defender = free space = plant.

# G. Anti-patterns (these lost recorded matches — never again)
- Same structure, new gadget ("place utility → get droned → fall back one
  layer → hold next crossing") — Gio has solved this script. Change the
  STRUCTURE (lateral re-entry, off-site start, second-crossing family).
- Full-map clears and 60s camera hunts on attack (IQ-vs-Valk round).
- Repeating the exact successful plan (Nomad decay, Gridlock-everywhere).
- Arriving at 0:30 with his stall utility intact (Goyo round).
- Paper-counters without a spatial plan (Zero-vs-Kaid round).
- Clash: excluded permanently by user preference.
