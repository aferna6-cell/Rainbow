## General 1v1 map principles (until per-map data exists)
- Small/medium maps (House, Chalet, Oregon, Clubhouse basement) compress
  the intel game — traps and sound beat cams. Big maps (Theme Park, Tower,
  Emerald Plains) reward roam-hunts and clock-squeezes.
- On any map: the defender should know the ONE soft floor above/below the
  objective (nitro/Buck line), and the attacker should know the TWO entries
  that can't be watched from one position.
- New map **Calypso** (Y11S2) — no data yet; first session there is
