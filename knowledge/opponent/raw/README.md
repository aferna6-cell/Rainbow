# Raw drop zone

Drop the gameplay-pattern snapshot here — any format: markdown notes, text
dumps, R6 Tracker screenshots/exports, VOD timestamp notes, old custom-GPT
conversation exports. Then tell the coach: **"ingest"**.

The coach reads everything, extracts opponent-behavior and self-behavior
claims into `../brother.md` with per-claim sources, and never modifies or
deletes files in this directory.

Useful things to capture when you make notes after playing:
- op he picked, side, site, where the fight happened, who peeked first
- what he did the round AFTER losing to something
- his bans, in order
