# 1v1 Siege — First Principles

Ranked meta is a prior, not an answer. A 1v1 custom match is a different game
from 5v5 and almost every public tier list mis-prices it. This file is the
coach's core theory; the pre-round call should be derivable from these
principles plus the opponent model.

## What changes from 5v5 → 1v1

| Axis | 5v5 value | 1v1 value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo intel (Jackal, Lion, Pulse, Dokkaebi rework) | good | **S-tier** | One ping = the whole enemy team located |
| Traps (Kapkan, Frost, Ela, Lesion, Melusi) | situational | **way up** | No teammate to spot/shoot traps or to trade after one connects |
| Intel denial (Vigil, Mute, Mozzie) | good | **way up** | Attacker usually has 1-2 drones total; killing/blocking them blinds him for the round |
| Hard breach (Thermite, Ace, Hibana) | essential | **way down** | No site-take with team utility; walls matter only for specific angles |
| Team-utility ops (Thatcher, Smoke plant-denial, Montagne) | high | **down** | Their value assumes teammates converting the space |
| Trade-dependent entry (Ash/Iana rush styles) | good | **risky** | Every death is round over — there is no trade |
| Sound | important | **dominant** | Only two people making noise; every step is attributable |

## The four pillars (every gameplan must answer all four)

1. **Information** — who finds whom first wins most rounds. Attacker: the
   drone that survives prep is worth more than a fast entry; drone, park it,
   move on the intel. Defender: cams, gadget pings, and sound traps
   (unreinforced hatches, shields in doorways, bulletproof cams) substitute
   for teammates' eyes.
2. **Time** — in bomb/secure the clock fights for the defender: attacker must
   eventually act. Attacker plans BACKWARD from the clock ("fight must start
   by 1:00"); defender plans to make every entry path cost seconds. If house
   rules are pure deathmatch (no objective), time pressure flips to whoever
   is more patient — say which applies in the call.
3. **Position** — pre-decide the fight you want: range (his gun vs yours),
   angle height, off-angle vs headhold, and a prepared rotation/repeal. The
   defender's superpower is fighting from a spot the attacker hasn't droned;
   the attacker's is choosing when and from which of two entries the fight
   starts.
4. **Psychology** — this is a repeated game vs one human who knows you.
   - Track his tendencies as frequencies; exploit ≥60% habits.
   - Assume level-1 adaptation: after you counter a habit once, he expects
     the counter. Mix.
   - Condition deliberately: spend a low-stakes round showing a pattern
     (e.g. always droning garage first), then break it on a big round.
   - Tilt is real data: after 2+ losses he gets faster and greedier —
     punish with traps and passive holds; after HE wins several, he repeats
     what worked — pre-aim it.

## Attacker frame (default skeleton)

1. Prep: both drones INTO the building, different halves. Goal by 0:45 of
   action phase: located him or eliminated half the map.
2. Keep one drone alive as your "teammate" — park it watching his rotate
   before you commit.
3. Default slow. Entry only when: located him, OR utility forced him to
   move, OR the clock says now.
4. Take map control silently (walk, close doors behind you matter less in
   1v1 — noise discipline matters more), establish one crossfire-free
   approach, then force the fight with utility (flash/candela/zofia
   concussion/grenade) rather than dry-peeking a warned defender.
5. Never re-peek the same angle you just lost a shot at; reposition first.

## Defender frame (default skeleton)

1. Decide BEFORE prep: anchor the objective, off-site hold (adjacent room,
   punish his approach), or roam-hunt his drones/entry. Say it in the call.
2. Spend prep on exactly two things: (a) intel net — cams kept alive,
   unreinforced walls as soundboards, gadgets on the two likeliest paths;
   (b) your ONE prepared fallback route, silent, to a second position.
3. Kill/deny drones above almost everything else. A blind attacker in a 1v1
   walks into crosshairs.
4. Never hold the position his drone saw you in. Move the moment you're
   spotted, arrive where he'll clear last.
5. C4/impacts are duel-enders: hold with the nitro on a soundcue spot
   (below-floor, behind soft wall) — in 1v1 nobody trades you afterward, so
   a utility kill is a free round.

## Ban logic in 1v1

- Ban target #1: his highest-confidence crutch op (opponent model).
- Ban target #2: the hard counter to YOUR planned strat (e.g. planning a
  Pulse nitro floor-hold → ban Zero/IQ-style scans if he uses them).
- Never burn a ban on "most banned in ranked" (Mira/Ace logic) — Mira in a
  1v1 without a coordinated site take is nowhere near her ranked value.
- Predict his ban: he bans what beat him last. Have the fallback pick READY
  in the call, same gameplan family if possible.

## Strategy families (rotate within a side, never streak one)

Attack: A1 slow-default (drone→locate→utility→fight) · A2 fast map-grab
(take his roam space before he settles, then slow) · A3 objective squeeze
(clock-weaponized, force him to come to you late) · A4 bait-entry (loud
fake at one entry, silent wrap through the other).

Defense: D1 anchor+net (traps and cams around obj, fight from prepared
angles) · D2 off-site ambush (hold the approach room he drones last) ·
D3 drone-hunt roam (kill intel early, then flank while he clears blind) ·
D4 vertical hold (below/above obj with nitro + soft-floor soundcues).

The call names the family and then makes it map-specific and
brother-specific. Two same-family rounds in a row max, and only after a win.
