# Session journal

One file per play session: `YYYY-MM-DD.md` (suffix `-2` for a second same-day
session). The coach creates and appends; treat as append-only during play.

Format:

```markdown
# Session 2026-08-27 · <map(s)> · rules: <mode/house rules>
Summary: (written at session end — score, what worked, what he adapted to,
top tendencies learned, opening plan for next time)

| R | Side | Ban (me) | Ban (him) | My op | His op | W/L | What happened |
|---|------|----------|-----------|-------|--------|-----|----------------|
| 1 | DEF  | Jackal   | Frost     | Vigil | Ash    | W   | killed his drones early, ambushed garage push |
```

"What happened" is one line, but name the rooms — it feeds the opponent
model and next round's call.
